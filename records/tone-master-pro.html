How it was learned

  1. First pair

    Two exports differing only in Greenbox 8 Drive revealed the scramble and the file’s structure.

  2. Definitions

    Control names, ranges and options were read from the definitions inside the owner’s copy of Pro Control.

  3. Calibration

    Four calibration presets set every knob to 37 per cent; photos of the unit’s screens confirmed the display rule.

  4. Linearity

    Two more presets checked 21 knobs at seven positions, including reversed and unit-bearing controls.

  5. Update

    After the firmware moved to 1.8.58, a preset re-saved on the unit matched the builder’s file exactly.

Limits

  • Presets use one straight signal chain.
  • Whether a preset sounds like the requested artist is judged by ear. The tests prove the file is correct, not that the tone is good.
  • Newer firmware may add blocks the record has not seen.

Use this skill

The skill is shared privately for now, because its control definitions come from Fender’s desktop app. Owners can ask for access.
